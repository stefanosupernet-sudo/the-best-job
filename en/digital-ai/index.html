Digital transformation and AI

We keep tools practical. Less copy-paste work, fewer version errors, a clearer view of customers, orders and stock—without buying technology for its own sake.

Concrete solutions

  • Websites & apps — clear on desktop and mobile; built for how you sell
  • Smart QR codes — product data, instructions and tracking where work happens
  • Product recognition — identify items or assets in seconds (Shazam-style utility)
  • CRM & automation — customers, orders and follow-ups in one flow
  • Price scraper — competitor prices monitored without living in spreadsheets
  • AI assistants — repetitive questions handled; people keep the judgement calls

What digital transformation means in practice

It is not “put everything in the cloud.” It is picking a few tools that pay for themselves, wiring them into what you already use, recovering hours, cutting errors and seeing the business in one place.

Does AI replace people?

No. It takes repetitive tasks. Your team stays on judgement, relationships and decisions. AI proposes and flags; you decide.

How we typically start

We map the process that costs the most time or mistakes, define a narrow first win, then expand only when the numbers make sense. Same approach whether you operate from Italy, Poland or both.
