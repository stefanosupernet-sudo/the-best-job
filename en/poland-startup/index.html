Startup support and market entry in Poland

Entering a new market is where most projects stall—not for lack of ambition, but for unclear next steps. We coordinate a practical package so international founders and SMEs can start in Poland with one contact, a clear sequence, and fewer surprises.

What the package can include

You pick what you need. Each item links to a dedicated service page.

  • Legal address & meeting rooms — a proper registered presence in Poland without leasing a full empty office from day one
  • Operational & admin support — local coordination of documents, follow-ups and day-to-day progress
  • Temporary manager — on-the-ground execution for a defined mandate, without a permanent hire before the model is proven
  • Market-entry strategy — demand, channels and priorities before you spend on structure
  • Permits & authority liaison — orientation on filings and sector requirements, sequenced with licensed professionals where required
  • Website & basic SEO — a clear online base for the Polish (or international) audience you want to reach

Who it is for

Founders, SME owners and corporate development teams from any country who want to invest, sell, produce or incorporate in Poland—whether you are setting up a sp. z o.o., testing a pilot, or building a commercial footprint.

We work in English, Italian and Polish. The goal is the same: a readable plan, local coordination, and decisions based on how business actually runs here—not on brochure language.

How we work

One primary contact. Written priorities. A sequence you can run. Where a Polish attorney, accountant or other licensed advisor is required, we say so up front and coordinate rather than pretend one person can hold every licence.

Typical first step: describe your product or service, the customer you want in Poland, and the outcome you need in 6–12 months. From there we propose a realistic scope—not a generic “enter Europe” slide deck.
